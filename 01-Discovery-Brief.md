# Discovery Brief

**Project:** Q3 2026 Bar & Lounge Lead-Generation Campaign
**Phase:** 1 — Discover
**Audience:** La Gaudière leadership (business stakeholder)
**Owner:** [Founder]
**Date:** 2026-05-10

---

## Goal (in business terms)

Win **4–6 new website-build clients** for La Gaudière over a 90-day campaign (June–August 2026), targeting bars, lounges, and restaurant-bars in Mauritius. Use the free website mockup as the primary conversion hook and the Digital Presence Starter as a stepping-stone offer for hesitant venues. Stack monthly retainers (paid ads / social) onto closed builds where the venue is a fit.

## How it works at a glance

We build a list of ~150 target venues across Mauritius. We reach them four ways: Instagram DMs, WhatsApp outreach, geo-targeted Meta ads, and in-person scouting. The hook is the same everywhere — *"Send us your Instagram on WhatsApp and we'll send back a free website mockup of your venue in 24 hours."* Mockup-takers are invited to a 20-minute discovery call. On the call we either close a Digital Presence Starter (MUR 4–5K) as a low-risk first project, or quote the full website build (MUR 45K+). Closed builds get pitched on the paid-ads or social retainer at the kickoff call.

## Success criteria

| Retainer add-ons attached | 2+ |

| Cost per signed build (paid + tools, ex. owner time) | < MUR 8K |

## Decisions needed

1. Sign-off on **MUR 60K paid-media budget** (Meta ads, 90 days).
2. Approval of the **free-mockup model** — ~2 hours of design time per mockup, used as the lead bait for any qualifying venue.
3. Confirmation that we have **delivery capacity** for 4–6 full builds plus 8–12 Starters in the same 90-day window.
4. Go/no-go on **in-person scouting** as a campaign channel — owner time, 1 evening/week.

## Top risks

- **Market size.** Mauritius has a finite count of qualifying venues. A campaign this aggressive could exhaust the addressable list in one or two passes.
- **Mockup fatigue.** Prospects may collect the mockup and disappear. Mitigation: tighten the qualification step before the mockup is built.
- **Owner bandwidth.** Scouting + mockup design + sales calls + delivery on one person is the chokepoint. Mitigation: cap mockups at 5/week, batch scouting on one evening.
- **Seasonality.** Q3 is the cooler season in Mauritius — possibly slower nightlife, possibly the right time to plan for summer. Mitigation: lean Q3 into "get ready for the high season" angle.
